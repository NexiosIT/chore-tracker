# Roadmap

## Current Phase

Phase 0 bootstrap.

## Themes

### T-01 — Kids get through their chores without being chased, with clear deadlines only where they matter

Some chores carry a timer set by a parent, and missing it halves the reward. Other chores are ad hoc: a kid can pick them up and complete them whenever they like, without being chased or penalised. Kids benefit from knowing exactly which chores are time-critical, and parents stop having to remind them about everything.

### T-02 — Every chore pays out fairly, with parents approving only what kids add themselves

When a parent created the chore, the kid's points are credited the moment it is completed. When a kid adds a chore of their own or logs a chore that nobody assigned, a parent approves it before points count. Points convert into pocket money or screen time at a rate the parents set, so kids always know what their effort is worth.

### T-03 — Parents and kids can see how each kid is doing over time

Parents can see how each kid is doing: which chores they completed this week and how this week compares to earlier weeks. Each kid can see the same picture for themselves. This gives the family a shared, factual view of effort instead of impressions.

### T-04 — Every family member has their own login, with parents in control of who joins

Parents and kids each register with a username and password. A kid can create their own account, but a parent approves the registration before it becomes active. This gives every action a clear owner and keeps the family space private.

### T-05 — The family can plan the week's chores at a glance

Kids and parents see chores laid out on a calendar-style dashboard. Parents can spot gaps or overloaded days, and kids can see what is coming up before it is due.

### T-06 — Nobody misses a chore or an approval because they weren't told

Kids receive in-app notifications when a chore is assigned to them or is about to be due. Parents receive notifications when a chore needs their approval or has been completed. This keeps timed chores from slipping and keeps approvals fast.
