# Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Family** | The single household the system serves. Nobody outside the family can see any of its data. |
| **Family member** | A parent or kid in the family, with their own username and password login. |
| **Parent** | A family member who creates and assigns chores, sets points and timers, keeps chore templates, approves kid-added chores and kid registrations, and sets the exchange rate. Parents can see all family data. |
| **Kid** | A family member who accepts, completes and adds chores, earns points, and redeems them. Kids see their own chores, points and reports, plus the family leaderboard. |
| **Registration** | A kid's sign-up request. It stays pending until a parent approves it, and only then can the kid log in. |
| **Chore** | A task worth a set number of points, optionally with a timer. It is assigned either to one kid or to a group of kids. |
| **Parent-created chore** | A chore a parent creates. Points are credited automatically when it is completed, with no approval step. |
| **Kid-added chore** | A chore a kid adds, including an ad-hoc chore they have already done. A parent approves it and sets its points before any points count. |
| **Ad-hoc chore** | A chore without a timer that a kid can complete whenever they like, without being chased or penalised. |
| **Timer** | An optional deadline a parent puts on a chore. If the chore is not completed before the timer runs out, its reward is halved. The chore stays open until the kid completes it or a parent removes it. |
| **Group assignment** | Assigning one chore to several kids at once. The first kid to take it gets it (first come, first served). |
| **Chore template** | A reusable chore definition with fixed points that parents use to create the same chore again later. |
| **Approval** | A parent's confirmation of a kid-added chore or of a kid's registration. |
| **Points** | The reward currency kids earn for completed chores and spend through redemptions. Also called coins. |
| **Points entry** | One change to a kid's points balance: a credit for a completed chore (half the reward if its timer was missed) or a deduction for a granted redemption. A balance can never go negative. |
| **Penalty** | The halving of a chore's reward when it is completed after its timer ran out. There are no negative points. |
| **Exchange rate** | The value of points in pocket money and in screen time, set by the parents. |
| **Redemption (cash-in)** | A kid's request to convert points into pocket money or screen time at the current exchange rate. It needs no parent approval, but the system refuses it if the kid does not have enough points. A parent marks it as granted once the reward has actually been given, and only then are the points deducted. |
| **Granted** | The state of a redemption once a parent has confirmed that the pocket money was paid or the screen time was given. Until then the redemption is outstanding and the kid's points are not yet deducted. |
| **Pocket money** | Money a kid receives by redeeming points. The app records the redemption only and stores no payment details. |
| **Screen time** | Extra device or screen minutes a kid receives by redeeming points. |
| **Streak** | Any calculated measure of sustained or record effort that encourages kids to complete chores. Examples: most chores in a week, most points in a week, or the most consecutive weeks in which points were earned. Streaks are calculated, not stored, and shown on the leaderboard. |
| **Leaderboard** | A ranking of the family's kids by points earned, available for different periods: all time, per week, per month, and so on. Streaks are shown alongside. It is calculated from completed chores and points entries. |
| **Notification** | An in-app message to a family member, such as chore assigned, chore due soon, chore needs approval, or chore completed. |
