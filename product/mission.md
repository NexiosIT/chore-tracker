# Mission

## Vision

Chore Tracker is a family app where kids complete chores, earn points, and swap those points for pocket money or screen time. Parents create chores, often from reusable templates with fixed points, and can put a timer on them. Kids can also propose chores of their own, which a parent approves before the work starts. Points are credited automatically as soon as an approved chore is completed. A weekly leaderboard and streaks make chores feel like a game rather than a negotiation.

## Problem Statement

Household chores in a family run on reminders, ad-hoc promises and memory. Nobody keeps a reliable record of who did what, so rewards like pocket money or extra screen time get handed out inconsistently and become a source of argument. Kids have no clear picture of what a chore is worth or how close they are to a reward. Parents spend their effort chasing and negotiating instead of setting expectations once. Chores with a deadline slip without any consequence.

## Target Audience

A single family, starting with our own household. It has two kinds of users:

- **Parents.** They create and assign chores, set a points value and an optional timer on each one, keep reusable chore templates, approve chores the kids propose, and set the exchange rate between points and pocket money or screen time. They mostly use the app on their phone, in short moments during the day.
- **Kids.** They see the chores assigned to them, accept and complete them, propose chores they want to do, track their points, streaks and leaderboard position, and cash in points for rewards.

Supporting more than one family is out of scope for now.

## Success Metrics

1. **Weekly streaks.** Every kid completes at least one chore each week, keeping their streak going.
2. **Fast parent approval.** Chores proposed by kids are approved or rejected within 24 hours.
3. **Points actually redeemed.** Kids regularly convert points into pocket money or screen time, which shows the reward loop works.
